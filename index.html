Recording Standby

Custom Note:

“Hi, this is [Exec] from Mindmatrix. Am I speaking with [Name]?”

If yes:
“Hi [First Name], how are you today?”
(Pause)

“I’m calling as part of our ongoing effort to ensure your success with Mindmatrix Bridge. We want to make sure everything is working smoothly, and that the platform continues to support your channel network effectively.

Do you have a few minutes? I’d love to hear how your experience has been so far and gather any feedback you might have.”

If they don't have time:

“I completely understand. I don’t want to keep you. Would there be a better time I can give you a quick call back?” (Note preferred time below)

If they won't commit:
“No problem. I can also send you a quick email with a short feedback link so you can share your thoughts at your convenience. Would that work better?”

📧 Send Follow-up Email (CC TL/Mgr)

“Hello [Name], this is [Exec] from Mindmatrix.”

“I’m reaching out to understand your experience with Mindmatrix Bridge and gather your feedback. Our aim is to ensure the platform is supporting you well in areas like onboarding, partner engagement, and automation.

When you get a moment, please review the email we sent and share your feedback. If you prefer to schedule a call, reply to the email or write to support@mindmatrix.net.

Thank you, and have a wonderful day!”
